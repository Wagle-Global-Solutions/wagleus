## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is prospective clients: businesses (so far in the UK, Australia, and elsewhere) evaluating Wagle Universal Solutions for custom web application development, SaaS product builds, or full-stack consulting. They are typically non-technical or semi-technical decision-makers assessing credibility and capability quickly, not casual browsers.

## Product Purpose

The site is the company's marketing/credibility surface: convince a visiting business to start a conversation about a project (custom web app, SaaS product, or consulting engagement) and make it easy to reach out. Secondary purpose is hosting 12 free developer tools (gradient generator, image resizer, PDF editor, etc.) as a value-add/lead channel, out of scope for this redesign pass.

## Positioning

"Built by engineers who ship products" — the team doesn't just contract-code, it has built and operates its own SaaS product (AstroEngine.ai) end to end, so it brings hands-on product-building experience (architecture, deployment, ongoing support) that a typical dev shop or freelancer roster can't truthfully claim.

## Product Principles

- Credibility over decoration: this is a Persuade surface for a small, real agency — trust signals (real shipped work, real founder, real contact) matter more than visual flourish.
- Proof through specificity: lean on the three named, verifiable projects rather than generic agency claims.
- Own-product credibility: AstroEngine.ai is the strongest differentiator (they build and run a SaaS, not just contract for one) and should be treated as a hero proof point, not a footnote.
- Small-team honesty: don't visually imply an enterprise agency scale the company doesn't have; confidence should read as competence, not size.
